PostgreSQL 16.14, shared_buffers=16MB, lsm.top_index_size=4MB, synchronous_commit=off
All processes on one small VM; the OS page cache absorbs much of the I/O,
so these numbers UNDERSTATE the LSM advantage on real storage.
